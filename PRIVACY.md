# Privacy policy for Deadline Tracker

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Deadline Tracker reads your deadlines from Waterloo Learn and Crowdmark and adds them to a calendar in your Google account.

## What it reads

- Your courses and calendar events from Learn
- Your upcoming assessments from Crowdmark

It reads these through your own Learn and Crowdmark tabs, using the login you already have in Chrome. It never asks for, sees or stores your passwords.

## What it stores

On your computer only:

- the deadlines it found (course, title, date, link)
- which ones you marked done
- which calendar event goes with which deadline

## Where your data goes

Only to Google Calendar, in a "Deadlines" calendar the extension makes. It can only see and change calendars it made, not the rest of your calendar. Nothing is sent to me or anyone else. No analytics, no tracking, no ads, and your data is never sold or shared.

Deadline Tracker's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

## Google sign-in

Uses Chrome's built-in Google sign-in. Chrome keeps the login, the extension never saves it. Sign out from the popup, or remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

## Deleting your data

Removing the extension deletes everything it stored on your computer. The "Deadlines" calendar stays until you delete it in Google Calendar.
